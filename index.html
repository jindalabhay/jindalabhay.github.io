About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, University of Ljubljana, working with Igor Klep, and head of the ARIS research project Mathematical Foundation of Bilinear Matrix Inequalities. I received my PhD from the Indian Institute of Science in 2024, advised by Tirthankar Bhattacharyya.

My research interests are operator theory, free analysis, noncommutative polynomial positivity and factorization, operator-valued Positivstellensätze, matrix convexity, operator algebras, and complete Nevanlinna–Pick spaces.
