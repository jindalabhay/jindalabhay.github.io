About

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, University of Ljubljana, working with Igor Klep. I received my PhD from the Indian Institute of Science in 2024, advised by Tirthankar Bhattacharyya.

I work in operator theory and its connections with function theory and noncommutative real algebraic geometry. Recent topics include complete Nevanlinna-Pick kernels and characteristic functions of operator tuples, operator-valued Positivstellensätze, and operator systems.
